WordShelf - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

WordShelf is a free app that helps people learn English with Urdu support. This page explains what information the app uses.

What we do not do

The app has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. We do not sell or share personal information.

Information stored on your device

The name you type on the first page, your scores, stars, streak and test marks are saved only on your own device (in the app's local storage). You can remove them by clearing the app's data.

Microphone and speech

The Speak and Self pages use the microphone only when you tap the microphone button. Speech is turned into text by your device or browser's speech service (for example, Google's speech recognition in Chrome), which may send your audio to that service to work. We do not record or keep your voice.

Translation

On the Self page, the text of what you said may be sent to a free online translation service (MyMemory) so that it can be shown in English. No name or other details are sent with it.

Fonts

The app may load fonts from Google Fonts. Your device may contact Google's servers to download them.

Children

The app is made for learners of all ages. It does not ask for personal details other than a name or nickname, which stays on the device. Please use a nickname instead of a full name.
